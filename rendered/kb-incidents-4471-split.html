Closing #4471 does not close one-day. Neither the parser fix nor retiring booking-v3 touches a date that moves by one day. Keep its ticket open.

How a report is placed, in this order (src/lib/kb/problems.ts):

  1. hint: it came in through the booking form of a tenant on booking-v3. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so people type the month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this.
  2. parser: every other report from a day-first tenant. The shared parser read the date month first, so day and month swapped.
  3. one-day: the desk read it as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Its dates are not flipped: the booking lands one day early, or before it was made, or on a different day per time zone. The desk’s own view counts these under parser; so does this table, and it lists them again here.

The tenant decides the problem, not intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and a reporter at a day-first tenant often has an en-US or en-GB browser.
